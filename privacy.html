workspace-cli

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 23, 2026

workspace-cli is a personal command-line research-file synchronization tool operated by the owner of the 0101son GitHub account. It is not offered as a public service.

Data accessed and purpose

With the owner's Google OAuth consent, rclone accesses Google Drive file contents and metadata, including names, identifiers, sizes and modification times, to list and download files from configured folders and upload local research state files to an output folder. The configured Drive permission is broad, although this workflow targets selected folders.

Storage and sharing

Downloaded files, synchronization state and logs are stored on the owner's computer/server; uploaded copies are stored in the owner's Google Drive. OAuth tokens and client configuration are stored locally by rclone. They are not published on this website. Google processes Drive and authorization requests under its policies. Research files are not intentionally published to GitHub by this tool. Google user data is not sold or used for advertising or to train generalized AI/ML models. The tool does not intentionally share it with third parties beyond the services needed for this synchronization, except as required by law.

Retention, deletion and control

Local and Drive copies remain until the owner deletes them. Revoking access does not delete existing copies. The owner can stop the synchronization service, remove local files and rclone credentials, delete Drive copies, and revoke workspace-cli access in Google Account settings at Google Account connections.

Google API data use

workspace-cli's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact and updates

Contact the operator through GitHub issues. Do not include private data or credentials in public issues. Changes to this policy will be published here with an updated effective date.
